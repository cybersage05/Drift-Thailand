Foreign Ownership

What foreigners
can own in Thailand.

🏢
Condo Freehold
Foreigners can own up to 49% of a condo building's total floor area. Freehold — you own it outright, no expiry date.
💵
FET Certificate
Funds must be transferred from overseas in foreign currency. Your bank issues an FET certificate — essential for foreign ownership registration.
🏨
Hotel Units
Hotel investment units can be owned freehold with managed rental agreements. Often comes with guaranteed rental yield from the developer.
📋
Leasehold Land
Foreigners cannot own land outright. But 30-year leases (renewable) are a common and legally sound structure for villas and houses.
